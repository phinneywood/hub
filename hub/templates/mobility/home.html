How this works & things to know

Same route, fair comparison

We calculate each riding leg and look for reachable buses. Walking transfers and scheduled waiting stay in the itinerary. No loading, unloading, pickup, parking or extra transfer allowances are added. Each walking comparison uses the same stops, scheduled bus departures and transfers as its riding option—only the first and last legs change to walking. If walking would miss the first bus, we show the earlier leave-by time, not a different bus or an impossible trip.

Check before you go

Bus times are estimates, not a guarantee of live tracking or rack space. Confirm operator bike policies and that your bike fits. A full rack may mean waiting for another bus. Cycling directions may include unsuitable or restricted roads. Scooter directions are a cycling proxy: check signs, legal routes and Lime’s app. End the rental before boarding; never carry a rented scooter onto the bus.

A useful search, not every possible route

Google transit alternatives and nearby corridor anchors at both ends provide candidates. Rail and ferries are excluded. We can miss better stops or connections. Separate Google Maps leg links can recalculate times. Minute labels are rounded individually; the total is calculated from the actual timestamps.

Your data & usage

Locations go to Google, not an AI provider. No trip history is saved. Only checking “Remember” stores the two locations in an owner-only local file on this Mac; Forget removes it. Route results are never saved. One search at a time, six attempts per hour; each search has a 24-request and 90-second ceiling. Sorting results makes no new API calls. Google quota settings—not budget alerts—limit account spending.
