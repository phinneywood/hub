Once a day, Hub will look for this sender’s inbox messages older than 30 days and archive up to 50. It uses Gmail’s sender search; messages marked with a star are excluded when it selects them.

This includes existing messages already older than 30 days. The first check is about 24 hours after you turn this on, and checks catch up when the Mac is running again. If more than 50 messages qualify, the rest wait for later checks.

Existing Gmail filters still apply and may cause new messages to skip the inbox immediately. This rule does not change those filters.
