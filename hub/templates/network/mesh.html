Optional Deco integration & limitations

amosyuen/ha-tplink-deco is a separate community Home Assistant integration, not a replacement for HA. It can publish client rates, node association and diagnostics where firmware supports them. BE11000 compatibility remains unverified here.

It requires Deco owner credentials; owner sessions can conflict, and device-list polling may briefly interrupt wireless backhaul on some firmware. Hub does not install it, authenticate to Deco, invoke control services or change polling. Diagnostic sensors may be disabled in HA. Do not interpret unavailable node data as a measured backhaul failure.
